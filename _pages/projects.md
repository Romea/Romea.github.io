## Research networks and facilities

TSCF participates in the [CIR ITPS — Innovative Transportation and Production Systems](https://cap2025.fr/fr/challenge2)
at Université Clermont Auvergne. Its Agrotechnologies theme connects robotics,
agricultural practices and the people who use these technologies. CapaRob is one
example of work supported by this research centre.

The [Roboterrium](https://roboterrium.hub.inrae.fr/) provides robots and experimental
facilities for research in natural and agricultural environments within the
[TIRREX infrastructure](https://tirrex.fr/). The
[AgroTechnoPôle](https://www.agrotechnopole.fr/) supports collaborative research,
testing and development with agricultural equipment partners.

Collaboration with the [Northern Robotics Laboratory (Norlab)](https://norlab.ulaval.ca/)
at Université Laval includes doctoral research on mapping and field robotics,
as well as joint studies of tracked vehicle motion.
