**Ph.D. project · Mohamed Ounally · Started November 2026**

An agricultural robot must place its tool accurately relative to the crop or
soil. Controlling the path of the mobile platform alone does not ensure that
the tool follows the desired motion. The tool position, actuator limits and
the motion of the platform have to be considered together.

This doctoral project studies constrained control for overactuated agricultural
robots. The objective is to coordinate the carrier and implement around the
point of agronomic action, making use of the available degrees of freedom
while limiting resource use and risks to crops. Mechanical weeding and
localized interventions are the first applications considered.

The work is carried out by [Mohamed Ounally](/people/mounally/) at INRAE and
Université Clermont Auvergne, under the supervision of Johann Laconte, Roland
Lenain, Riccardo Polvara and Florian Rançon. It is supported by the
[PEPR Agroécologie et Numérique programme](https://www.pepr-agroeconum.eu/eng/calls-for-projects/doc-postdoc-call-for-projects/winning-projects-of-the-2026-doctorate-and-postdoctorate-call).
