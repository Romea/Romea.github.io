If you would like to join R-LEAF, start with our [research projects](/research/)
and [publications](/publications/). We work on robots operating in agricultural
and natural environments, with projects in control, navigation, mapping and
robot interaction with vegetation and soil.

## Internships and doctoral research

Master's internships usually last five to six months. A Ph.D. normally takes
three years. The research question, experimental work and expected outputs
should be discussed with the supervisor before the project starts.

Funding, salary, travel support and doctoral training depend on the position
and contract. Ask about these when discussing an advertised opening. The
[doctoral school guide](https://dred.uca.fr/etudes-doctoralesbr-hdr/etudes-doctorales/espace-des-doctorants/guide-du-doctorant)
and [INRAE guide for international scientists](https://guide-for-international-scientists.inrae.fr/working-at-inrae/contractual-phd-students/undertaking-a-phd-in-france/)
provide further information.

## Research visits

If you are already enrolled in a Ph.D. elsewhere, a visit can be a way to work
on a shared question. Explain how your work relates to the laboratory and what
you would like to accomplish during the visit. Allow time for the scientific
and administrative arrangements before making travel plans.

## How to contact us

Write to [Johann Laconte](/contact/) with a short description of your interests,
your academic CV and the dates you have in mind. Mention the project or paper
that prompted your interest. If you have identified a funding programme,
include that information too.
