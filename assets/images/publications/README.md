# Publication previews

The previews reuse figures and photographs already used on the research pages.
They are resized, compressed and stripped of metadata for the web.

These two figures are available under CC BY 4.0, with author credits on the site:

- `feeling-the-force.webp`: Khizar et al., robot photograph, https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19831
- `localization.webp`: Dannaoui et al., evolving environment illustration, https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17531

Other previews are web copies of existing photographs:

- `implement-control.webp`: `research/control_implement/full.jpg`
- `implement-predictive.webp`: `research/control_implement_mpc/full.jpg`
- `traversability.webp`: `research/traversability_philippe/robot.jpg`
- `energy.webp`: `research/energy/topview_1200x675.jpg`
- `energy-slopes.webp`: `research/energy_slopes/robot_scenario_1200x675.jpg`

Photographs fill their preview frames; annotated illustrations remain intact.
