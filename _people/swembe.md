Stéphane Ngnepiepaye Wembe worked on control and model predictive control for
agricultural robots at INRAE from April 2023 to April 2026, in collaboration with
[Sabi Agri](https://sabi-agri.com/). His research investigated trajectory tracking
for agricultural implements under the main supervision of Roland Lenain.
He defended his Ph.D. at Université Clermont Auvergne on 19 May 2026.
