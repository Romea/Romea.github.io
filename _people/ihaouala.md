**Ines Haouala** is a doctoral researcher at [INRAE](https://www.inrae.fr/en),in collaboration with [Université Clermont Auvergne](https://www.uca.fr/). She is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions scholarship holder. Her research focuses on adaptive navigation and traversability for agriculture robots on uncertain terrains.

SHe holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Power Systems Engineering from Glasgow Caledonian University in collaboration with African Leadership University. Then, she completed her Master's Degree in Robotics Engineering in Italy at [Univerisy of Genoa](https://unige.it/en). During her Master's, she received the Extra-EU Erasmus scholarship to complete her final thesis project in autonomous navigation for agriculture application at the [University of California, Merced](https://www.ucmerced.edu/). 

# Education

- **Ph.D. (Robotics)** - INRAE (in collaboration with Université Clermont Auvergne), France/Portugal - Ongoing
- **M.Sc.** in **Robotics Engineering** - University of Genoa, Italy, 2025
- **B.Eng.** in **Electrical Power Systems Engineering** - Glasgow Caledonian University in collaboration with African Leadership University, Mauritius, 2021
